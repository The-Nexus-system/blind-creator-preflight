Bring a post to Pre-Flight

Pre-Flight cannot step inside Facebook or Instagram while you compose — those apps give no outside app a pre-post doorway, and the phone keeps each app's contents to itself. What Pre-Flight can do is stand at the sharing step, where the photo is already moving between apps:

  • Share a photo to Pre-Flight. On Android and other browsers that support installed web apps in the Share sheet, install Pre-Flight below and it appears as a Share choice for photos. Pick it and the photo lands in “Check your own photo,” already checked.
  • Start here, then share onward. Check the photo below, approve the words, and the Share step at the end sends the checked photo and its alt text onward — to Facebook or Instagram where they appear in your share sheet.
  • On iPhone and iPad, Apple does not give web apps a place in the Share sheet. Open Pre-Flight and pick the photo, or set the posting prompt below so your phone nudges you before you post.

Installing is optional — Pre-Flight works in this browser tab either way. Installing is what gives it a home-screen icon and, where the browser supports it, its place in the Share sheet.

Posting prompt (iPhone and iPad)

A Shortcut automation can ask “Posting? Run Pre-Flight first?” whenever Facebook or Instagram opens. It cannot see what you pick inside those apps — it is a nudge at the door, not a step inside. To set it up:

  1. Copy the Pre-Flight link with the button above.
  2. Open the Shortcuts app, go to Automation, and add a new personal automation.
  3. Choose App, pick Facebook or Instagram, and choose “Is Opened.”
  4. Add the action “Show Notification” with the words: Posting? Run Pre-Flight first.
  5. Add the action “Open URLs” and paste the Pre-Flight link.

Check your own photo

Pick a photo of your own and the same six checks run on it, live. Your browser converts the photo you choose to PNG and sends it to be checked; Pre-Flight keeps a working copy of it on the server while you check, repair, and share it, and that copy is deleted within two hours. A photo of your own has no recorded facts and no stated intent, so where its subject cannot be located, framing and crop safety will say uncertain and say why.

Any photo your browser can open works — HEIC, JPEG, or PNG; it is converted to PNG in the browser before it is sent. Photos over the 15 MB limit are scaled down first.

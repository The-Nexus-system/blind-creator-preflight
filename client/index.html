Check a photo or a short video before you post it. Every result is plain text: pass, fix, or uncertain.

Check your own photo

Pick a photo of your own and the same six checks run on it, live. Your browser converts the photo you choose to PNG and sends it to be checked; Pre-Flight keeps a working copy of it on the server while you check, repair, and share it, and that copy is deleted within two hours. A photo of your own has no recorded facts and no stated intent, so where its subject cannot be located, framing and crop safety will say uncertain and say why.

Any photo your browser can open works — HEIC, JPEG, or PNG; it is converted to PNG in the browser before it is sent. Photos over the 15 MB limit are scaled down first.
